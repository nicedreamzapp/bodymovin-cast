# Wolf Strike sound effects

Every sound in Wolf Strike is a real recording from Wikimedia Commons, cut and leveled with ffmpeg (peaks -1 dBFS,
short fades so nothing clicks; loops crossfaded 1 s). Cut files are shared under the same license as their source. No sound is synthesized.

- uzi_shot1/2/3.mp3 (three single rounds), uzi_tail.mp3 (the echo after a burst): from "Palm kazerne, Uzi machinegeweer - SoundCloud - Beeld en Geluid.ogg" by Beeld en Geluid (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3APalm_kazerne%2C_Uzi_machinegeweer_-_SoundCloud_-_Beeld_en_Geluid.ogg), CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0).
- rifle1.mp3 (enemy rifle shot): from "FN FAL, een schot - SoundCloud - Beeld en Geluid.ogg" by Beeld en Geluid (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AFN_FAL%2C_een_schot_-_SoundCloud_-_Beeld_en_Geluid.ogg), CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0).
- rifle2.mp3 (enemy rifle shot): from "FN FAL, drie schoten - SoundCloud - Beeld en Geluid.ogg" by Beeld en Geluid (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AFN_FAL%2C_drie_schoten_-_SoundCloud_-_Beeld_en_Geluid.ogg), CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0).
- mag_in.mp3 (magazine in), bolt.mp3 (bolt / grenade launch), click.mp3 (empty click, buttons): from "Magazijn laden en het vergrendelen van een geweer - SoundCloud - Beeld en Geluid.ogg" by Beeld en Geluid (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AMagazijn_laden_en_het_vergrendelen_van_een_geweer_-_SoundCloud_-_Beeld_en_Geluid.ogg), CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0).
- shot_far1/2.mp3 (distant shots, a hit on you): from "Gunshots 8.ogg" by aradlaw (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AGunshots_8.ogg), Public domain.
- boom_big.mp3 (every explosion, pitched per size): from "Explosion 10.ogg" by tcpp (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AExplosion_10.ogg), Public domain.
- heli_loop.mp3 (helicopters, seamless loop): from "Chinook helicopter flying over Greenwich in London.ogg" by JoBrodie (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AChinook_helicopter_flying_over_Greenwich_in_London.ogg), CC0 (http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/deed.en).
- boat_loop.mp3 (patrol boats, seamless loop): from "WWS MSThti.ogg" by Work With Sounds / Werstas (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AWWS_MSThti.ogg), CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0).
- engine_loop.mp3 (trucks and armoured cars, seamless loop): from "Detroit62.ogg" by Sixthstar (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3ADetroit62.ogg), CC0 (http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/deed.en).
- jungle.mp3 (the ambience bed; the music button silences it): from "Walk in the rainforest.ogg" by ezwa (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AWalk_in_the_rainforest.ogg), Public domain.
