Body Movin' privacy policy

Body Movin' for iPhone, Body Movin' for Android (Google Play) and Body Movin' for Fire TV, published by Nice Dreamz LLC. Last updated November 12, 2026.

The short version

Body Movin' does not collect any data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing about you is sent to us or to anyone else.

The camera

The app uses your phone's camera (iPhone or Android) to follow your body while you play. The picture is processed on the phone itself to work out where your joints are. Video is never recorded, saved or sent over the internet.

What travels over your Wi-Fi

When you play on a TV, computer or tablet, the games run from your phone. The phone sends your joint positions, and if the game shows one, a small live picture of you in the corner of the screen, straight to the screen you chose on your own Wi-Fi network. None of it leaves your home network, and none of it is stored.

When you use AirPlay or Screen Mirroring, Apple's own system feature carries the picture to your TV. When you open the game in a web browser, that browser loads the game from your phone, not from a website.

Body Movin' for Android

The Android app works the same way: the camera picture is processed on the phone and never recorded or sent, and the games run from the phone over your own Wi-Fi. A TV or computer gets your moves only when it opens the link your phone gives it (the link carries a long random code, or you type the short code the phone shows), so no other device on the network can watch. The game pictures and sounds are installed with the app by Google Play; the app itself downloads nothing from us and sends nothing to us.

Body Movin' for Fire TV

The Fire TV app is the screen end of the game. It has no camera or microphone access, no account and no analytics. While it is open it tells phones on your Wi-Fi the TV's name so your iPhone can find it, and when you pick it on your iPhone it shows the game your phone serves over your home Wi-Fi. Its waiting screen is built into the app and loads nothing from the internet. It does not collect, store or send any personal information.

What the app keeps on your phone

A few settings, such as whether the voice is on. They stay on your phone and are removed when you delete the app.

Other companies

Body Movin' contains no advertising, analytics or crash reporting software. To play on a Chromecast or Google TV the app uses Google's Cast library, which starts only when you open the Pick your TV list; the game then goes from your phone to that TV over your Wi-Fi. On Android, Google Play delivers the app's larger pictures after install. Neither sends us anything about you.

Children

Body Movin' is a family game and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
