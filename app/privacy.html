Body Movin' privacy policy

Body Movin' for iPhone, published by Nice Dreamz LLC. Last updated November 3, 2026.

The short version

Body Movin' does not collect any data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing about you is sent to us or to anyone else.

The camera

The app uses your iPhone's camera to follow your body while you play. The picture is processed on the phone itself to work out where your joints are. Video is never recorded, saved or sent over the internet.

What travels over your Wi-Fi

When you play on a TV, computer or tablet, the games run from your iPhone. The phone sends your joint positions, and if the game shows one, a small live picture of you in the corner of the screen, straight to the screen you chose on your own Wi-Fi network. None of it leaves your home network, and none of it is stored.

When you use AirPlay or Screen Mirroring, Apple's own system feature carries the picture to your TV. When you open the game in a web browser, that browser loads the game from your phone, not from a website.

What the app keeps on your phone

A few settings, such as whether you have seen the welcome screens. They stay on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app.

Other companies

Body Movin' contains no advertising, analytics or crash reporting software. The app includes Google's Cast library for a future Chromecast option. In this version it is switched off and never started, so it does not run or send anything.

Children

Body Movin' is a family game and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
