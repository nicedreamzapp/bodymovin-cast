Body Movin' support

Motion games you play with your whole body. Your iPhone is the camera and the game console.

Getting set up

  1. Stand your iPhone upright about 6 to 8 feet away, back camera facing you.
  2. Step back until it sees you from head to knees. The banner at the top turns green when it sees you.
  3. Pick a screen to play on (below).

Playing on a TV, computer or tablet

Web browser. Any smart TV, computer or tablet with a web browser works. Put it on the same Wi-Fi as your iPhone, open its browser and type the address the app shows (it looks like 192.168.1.20:8080). The game runs from your phone, nothing goes over the internet.

AirPlay. On an Apple TV or an AirPlay TV, open Control Center on the iPhone, tap Screen Mirroring and pick the TV. The game fills the TV and the phone becomes the remote.

On the phone. Tap "Try the games on this phone" to play right on the iPhone.

If something isn't working

  • The browser can't open the address: make sure both are on the same Wi-Fi network, not a guest network, and that you allowed Body Movin' to use the local network (iPhone Settings, Privacy and Security, Local Network).
  • The banner stays red: step back so your whole body is in view, add some light, and keep the phone still.
  • Moves feel late: move the phone closer to the Wi-Fi router, or use the web browser path instead of mirroring.
  • Want to look around without standing up? Tap "Sample player" and a recorded player plays the games for you.
